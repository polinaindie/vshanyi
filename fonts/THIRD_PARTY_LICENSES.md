# Third-party fonts

The following typefaces are included as free-use fonts. Keep this notice with redistributed font files and verify the linked terms before commercial deployment.

- **Mariupol Regular / Medium / Bold** — designed by Andrij Shevchenko and Sergiy Rodionov. Listed as free: https://rentafont.com/fonts/mariupol
- **Kharkiv Tone Regular** — designed by Kateryna Drozd. Free for personal and commercial use: https://fontesk.com/kharkiv-tone-font/
- **NASTUP Basic** — designed by Maksym Kobuzan. Free for personal and commercial use: https://www.behance.net/gallery/152356721/NASTUP-BASIC-Free-Font-Cyrillic-Latin-Greek
